    Common Questions

    FREQUENTLY ASKED
    QUESTIONS.

    Does the dental implant procedure hurt?

    No. Implant placement is done under local anaesthesia and most patients report only mild soreness afterwards, easily managed with regular painkillers.

    How long does a root canal take?

    Most root canals at Doon Smiles are completed in a single 45–60 minute sitting using modern rotary endodontic technology.

    Can my existing dental panel support an EV charger?

    Most modern 150 or 200-amp dental panels can support a Level 2 EV charger. Older 60 or 100-amp panels usually require a service panel upgrade first.

    Do you handle dental emergencies?

    Yes — for severe pain, knocked-out teeth or trauma, call our emergency line and we will arrange a same-day appointment.
